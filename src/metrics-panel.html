What these numbers cover

The source is /api/gallery, the published archive index. Each model and job ID is counted once. Completion times come from the worker’s finished time, with receipt creation time as a fallback. Pending uploads, queued work, failed attempts and unarchived generations are outside this record.

This view checks every 15 seconds while its tab is open and visible. A connection problem keeps the last good snapshot. Times use your browser’s local time zone.
